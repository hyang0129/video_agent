# Topic Identification for Market Research

## Overview

This document outlines an agentic pipeline for identifying content opportunities by analyzing the gap between long-form and short-form content on YouTube. The goal is to discover topics that have strong engagement in long-form content but lack representation in the short-form space.

## Topic vs Sub-Topic Hierarchy

### Topics (Channel-Level)
Topics represent the broad thematic focus of a channel. A channel should be restricted to a single topic to maintain consistency and build a targeted audience.

**Characteristics:**
- Broad enough to sustain multiple sub-topics
- Specific enough to define a clear niche
- Represents the channel's core identity

**Examples:**
- Sci-Fi Facts
- History Mysteries
- Tech Explainers
- True Crime Stories
- Space Discoveries
- Movie Analysis

### Sub-Topics (Content-Level)
Sub-topics are specific areas to explore within the overarching topic. Each video or content series focuses on a particular sub-topic.

**Characteristics:**
- Narrow, specific angles within the main topic
- Can be exhausted in a single video or short series
- Multiple sub-topics feed into the parent topic

**Examples (for "Sci-Fi Facts"):**
- Star Wars Facts
- Star Trek Lore
- Dune Universe Explained
- Blade Runner Easter Eggs
- Sci-Fi Predictions That Came True

## Content Identification Criteria

### Long-Form Content Patterns

We're specifically targeting informative, curiosity-driven content formats that perform well in long-form but could be adapted to short-form:

#### 1. "Did You Know" Format
- Lesser-known facts and trivia
- Surprising information about familiar subjects
- Educational revelations

**Indicators:**
- Titles containing "Did You Know", "Facts You Didn't Know"
- High engagement on informative content
- Comment sections showing surprise/learning

#### 2. "You Won't Believe" Format
- Shocking or counterintuitive information
- Dramatic reveals and discoveries
- Mind-blowing statistics or comparisons

**Indicators:**
- Titles with "You Won't Believe", "Shocking", "Mind-Blowing"
- Strong emotional reactions in comments
- High share rates

#### 3. "Top X About Y" Format
- List-based content (Top 10, Top 5, etc.)
- Ranked information
- Curated compilations

**Indicators:**
- Titles with "Top", "Best", "Most", numbered lists
- High retention rates (viewers watching full lists)
- Playlist-able content

## Market Research Pipeline

### Phase 1: Topic Discovery
1. **Scan Long-Form Space**
   - Identify channels with high view counts (100K+ views)
   - Focus on informative/educational content
   - Track engagement metrics (likes, comments, watch time)
   - Categorize by topic area

2. **Analyze Content Patterns**
   - Extract common themes
   - Identify recurring sub-topics
   - Note content formats (Did You Know, Top X, etc.)

### Phase 2: Gap Analysis
1. **Survey Short-Form Space**
   - Search for same topics in Shorts/TikTok/Reels
   - Measure content density (how many creators cover it)
   - Assess engagement levels

2. **Identify Gaps**
   - Topics with high long-form performance but low short-form presence
   - Under-served sub-topics
   - Format opportunities (long-form lists that could become short clips)

### Phase 3: Opportunity Scoring

Score potential topics based on:
- **Long-form demand**: View counts, engagement, channel growth
- **Short-form gap**: Low competition, few existing creators
- **Format compatibility**: Can the content be effectively shortened?
- **Evergreen potential**: Will it remain relevant over time?
- **Production feasibility**: Can we create quality content in this area?

### Phase 4: Sub-Topic Mapping

For each identified topic:
1. List all discovered sub-topics
2. Prioritize by:
   - Search volume
   - Competition level
   - Production difficulty
   - Trend trajectory (rising vs declining)
3. Create content calendar

## Repackaging Opportunities

### From Long-Form to Short-Form

Many successful long-form videos can be deconstructed into short-form content:

#### List Videos → Individual Shorts
- "Top 10 Sci-Fi Facts" becomes 10 individual shorts
- Each fact is self-contained
- Cross-reference and drive traffic between pieces

#### Deep Dives → Highlight Clips
- Extract most surprising moments
- Create "hook" content from key reveals
- Use as lead-in to longer content

#### Explainers → Quick Tips
- Break down complex topics into bite-sized pieces
- Series format: Part 1, Part 2, etc.
- Build anticipation for full explanation

### Integration with Video Creation Pipeline

**Input to Pipeline:**
- Topic area (validated through market research)
- Sub-topic for specific video
- Content format (Did You Know, Top X, etc.)
- Source inspiration (if repackaging)

**Benefits:**
- Data-driven topic selection
- Reduced creative risk
- Built-in audience validation
- Multiple content pieces from single research effort

## Monitoring and Iteration

### Success Metrics
- View counts on created content
- Engagement rates (likes, comments, shares)
- Follower growth rate
- Cross-platform performance

### Feedback Loop
1. Track performance of created content
2. Identify top-performing sub-topics
3. Double down on successful areas
4. Retire under-performing sub-topics
5. Discover new sub-topics within successful topics

### Adaptation Strategy
- Monthly review of topic performance
- Quarterly reassessment of topic selection
- Stay alert to trending sub-topics
- Balance evergreen and trending content
